# Son Sipariş — Game Vision

## Game fantasy

Son Sipariş lets the player feel like the fast, inventive owner of a tiny restaurant during a busy shift. Orders arrive above a crowded work surface; the player improvises a compact kitchen, combines ingredients, and delivers food before the moment passes.

## Target audience

The MVP targets mobile players who enjoy readable, tactile card interactions, light roguelite decision-making, short repeatable sessions, and the satisfaction of optimizing a small workspace. It should be approachable for casual players while leaving room for mastery through faster layouts, clean stacks, and combo chains.

## Core gameplay loop

1. An order appears with a clear recipe target.
2. The player arranges ingredient and equipment cards on the shared kitchen table.
3. Ingredients are processed or combined into intermediate and finished food cards.
4. The finished card is dragged to the service counter.
5. The order pays coins and advances the combo.
6. A new order creates another opportunity to improve the table layout and flow.

The first playable loop is intentionally narrow: cook **Köfte** on **Tava**, combine **Ekmek**, **Pişmiş Köfte**, and **Peynir** into **Klasik Burger**, then serve it.

## Differentiating features

- **A freeform kitchen table:** preparation is spatial and player-authored, not a sequence of locked production lanes.
- **Card combinations with physical intent:** proximity, stacking, and equipment placement express cooking actions with simple interactions.
- **Compact-layout mastery:** players create efficient micro-kitchens and discover faster hand movements.
- **Short-shift momentum:** orders, coin rewards, and combo progression create a satisfying cadence without requiring a long session.

## The freeform kitchen table

The central table is a single, generous shared play space. Ingredient cards, equipment cards, and later card stacks can be placed anywhere within it. Cards softly snap to an invisible grid so the table remains legible, but the player is never forced into vertical lanes or prescribed slots. A good layout is a player decision: ingredients may sit beside a pan, be stacked as preparation progresses, or be grouped for the next recipe.

## Player motivation and reward moments

The immediate reward is the transformation of a simple card into a prepared ingredient or finished dish. Serving a correct order delivers a second reward through coins, a visible combo increase, and a freshly arriving order. Over repeated short shifts, the player should feel their speed, layout choices, and recipe fluency improving.
